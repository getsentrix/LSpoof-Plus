How to install

Takes less than two minutes. No jailbreak or computer cable needed.

Download Latest .dylib ↗
01

Download LocationSpoofer.dylib

Grab LocationSpoofer.dylib from the latest GitHub release onto your iPhone or computer.

02

Inject it into your app

In LiveContainer, put the .dylib file in the app's Tweaks folder and turn it on. In Sideloadly, add it under Advanced Options before installing the IPA.

03

Hold three fingers to open

Open your app, press and hold three fingers anywhere on the screen for 0.8 seconds, choose your location, and tap Save Settings.

Questions

Do I need a jailbreak or a computer plugged in? +

No. Once the file is added to your app using LiveContainer, TrollStore, or Sideloadly, everything runs right on your iPhone. You never need to plug into a PC or Mac.

Does this change my location for my whole iPhone? +

No. Because iOS keeps apps separate, LSpoof+ only changes the location for the specific app you add it to. Your real Apple Maps, Find My, and system GPS stay normal.

How do I hide or reopen the menu? +

Tap the × button in the top-right corner to hide it. Whenever you want it back, hold three fingers anywhere on the screen for 0.8 seconds.
