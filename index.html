Straightforward Answers

Frequently Asked Questions.

Does LSpoof+ require a jailbroken iPhone or computer tether?
No. LSpoof+ runs entirely in user-space inside sideloaded apps. You only need a sideloading manager such as LiveContainer, TrollStore, or Sideloadly. No computer cables, no tethering daemons, and no jailbreak required.
Does this spoof system-wide GPS or just my target app?
Because modern iOS securely sandboxes sideloaded apps, LSpoof+ intercepts location calls specifically for the application it is injected into (such as Pokémon GO, Life360, Tinder, Find My, or social tools). Your device's native Apple Maps and emergency GPS remain untouched.
How do I dismiss and reopen the menu inside my app?
Tap the ✕ button in the top right to instantly hide the overlay. To summon the spoofer menu at any point, hold three fingers on your screen simultaneously for 0.8 seconds.
